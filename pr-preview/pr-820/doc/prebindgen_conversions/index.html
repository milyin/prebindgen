Reusable boundary conversions and their representation metadata.

These helpers build InputPlan and OutputPlan values. They do not interpret declarations or select functions to export. Adapters may use the helpers during preparation and their wire metadata during generation.

Generated code calls prebindgen-runtime at runtime; this crate itself is used only during binding generation.
