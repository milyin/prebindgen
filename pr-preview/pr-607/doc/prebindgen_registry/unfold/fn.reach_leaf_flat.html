Expand description

Fold a leaf’s whole path over base with no optional-step handling, then apply the terminal treatment the leaf calls for: a leaf reached by a field read is cloned out of the place it reached, because its converter takes the field type as written (owned); every other leaf keeps the borrow its converter expects.

One derivation, used by every delivery an adapter renders and by the single-leaf shortcut a decomposed return takes. They drifted once while they were two — the shortcut was missing the field clone and handed &F to an F converter — which is why the walk is the registry’s.
