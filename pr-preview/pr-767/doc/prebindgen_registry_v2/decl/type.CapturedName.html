A captured item by name: one of the three kinds the source captures, and what it is called there.

Less than a Declaration, deliberately. An ignore names such an item and says nothing about how the target would get it, so it cannot be a callback, a conversion or anything the binding coined — those are not captured, and there is nothing in the source to leave alone.
