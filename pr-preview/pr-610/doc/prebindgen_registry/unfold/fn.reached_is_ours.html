pub fn reached_is_ours<L: DecomposedLeaf>(leaf: &L, consuming: bool) -> bool
Expand description

Whether what a leaf reaches is OURS, and so is moved rather than borrowed or cloned. The two leaf kinds say it differently:

  • an IDENTITY leaf carries the answer in its own reading — the plan resolved that to the owned type exactly when the value is the plan’s to give away (an owned root, or a field of a CONSUMING value form), and that is also what selected the owning converter, which boxes the move rather than cloning a borrow;
  • every other leaf reads a field, whose reading is the field type as written and owned either way, so ownership is the enclosing form’s: only a consuming one gives its fields away.
