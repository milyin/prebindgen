Module record 

Source
Expand description

Records — structs and the alternatives of a sum — taken apart into their fields and put back together.

Two uses, one mechanism:

  • Decomposition (record_in, record_out): the record crosses as the concatenation of its fields’ wires, with no type of its own on the boundary. Recursing into a field that is itself a record gives the leaf-by-leaf crossing a JVM binding uses.
  • Mirrors (StructWriter, SumWriter): the record crosses as a generated type whose fields are the wires — a #[repr(C)] struct or enum for C — plus the two conversions between it and the source type.

Either way the adapter decides, per field, through FieldCallbacks; the helpers here only handle the Rust shape of the record: named or positional fields, the delimiters the source wrote, the order.
